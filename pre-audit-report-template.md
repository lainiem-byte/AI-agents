# LNL Pre-Audit Report Template

> This Pre-Audit Report is the centerpiece of the 30-minute call. It transitions the prospect from "sharing files" to "seeing the solution."
>
> In Notion, create this as a **Page Template** inside each industry Vault database. When a client uploads their docs, apply this template to their page and fill it out before the meeting.

---

## 1. Executive Summary: The Leakage Score

| Metric | Score |
|--------|-------|
| **Creative Authority Score** | /10 (visual consistency and scroll-stop ability) |
| **Operational Efficiency Score** | /10 (tech stack integration and manual touchpoints) |

**The North Star Objective:** A one-sentence summary of what LNL will solve (e.g., "Reclaiming 15 billable hours/month through Invisible Associates").

---

## 2. Industry-Specific Findings

### For LNL Creative (Med Spa / Real Estate)

- **Visual Friction:** Identify where high-end clients are being turned off by low-quality assets or inconsistent branding.
- **Conversion Gaps:** Pinpoint the exact moment a lead stops clicking in the current funnel.
- **Cinematic Potential:** Highlight 1-2 raw clips the client uploaded and explain the "LNL Refresh" you'd apply to them.

### For LNL Automations (Law / Home Services)

- **Manual Bottlenecks:** List the specific tasks (Intake, Invoicing, Dispatch) currently consuming human time.
- **Tech Overlap:** Identify "Ghost Apps" they are paying for that duplicate functionality in their stack.
- **Speed-to-Lead Audit:** Show exactly how many minutes/hours pass before a lead gets a response vs. the industry standard (under 5 mins).

---

## 3. The Future State Map

- **Phase 1 (Quick Win):** The one automation or creative fix that pays for itself in 30 days.
- **Phase 2 (Scaling):** The long-term system build-out (e.g., Full CRM Automation or Cinematic Ad Campaigns).

---

## Vault Client Checklist (Site Copy)

### Med Spa & Real Estate

- **Logo & Brand Kit:** High-resolution files for visual alignment audit.
- **Active Ad Creative:** Screenshots or files of currently running Meta/Google ads.
- **Top 3 Social Posts:** Best-performing content for hooks/engagement analysis.

### Law & Home Services

- **Software Inventory:** A list or screenshot of all monthly tech subscriptions.
- **Intake/Lead Logs:** An export of last 10 leads to audit Speed to Lead.
- **Sample Service Agreement:** A blank contract or invoice for document-automation review.
